Real Madrid, told in eras

This is a history of a football club rather than a list of trophies. It runs in six periods, from a side put together by students in 1902 to the team that lifted a fifteenth European Cup in 2024 — who played, how they played, and what changed.

Everything here is written from sources I can point to, and where a number is disputed I say so rather than picking the flattering one.

Blanco — an independent student project. Not affiliated with Real Madrid CF.
