Fifteen finals, fifteen wins

Real Madrid have played fifteen European Cup finals and won all fifteen. That record is the most useful single fact about the club: the longer the competition runs, the more likely they are to be the team still standing at the end.

The competition was the European Cup until 1992 and the Champions League after that. The table below lists every final, with the opponent, the score and where it was played.
