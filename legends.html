Six players who define the eras

A club with this many great players cannot be summarised by a list of the best ones. These six were chosen for what they changed about the club, not for how many medals they collected — which is why some obvious names are missing.

Alfredo Di Stéfano

1953–1964 · Forward

The player the first great team was built around, and the reason five consecutive European Cups became possible. He scored in all five of those finals — a record nobody has approached since.

Ferenc Puskás

1958–1966 · Forward

Signed at 31 and still good enough to score four goals in the 1960 final. If Di Stéfano was the team's brain, Puskás was the left foot that finished the argument.

Francisco Gento

1953–1971 · Winger

The only player to have won six European Cups. He played in the 1956 final and was still running the left wing when the club won the sixth, ten years later.

Raúl González

1994–2010 · Forward

The bridge between the Galácticos and the academy. He left as the club's record appearance maker and, at the time, its record goalscorer — both set by a player who never had a season like the ones above.

Cristiano Ronaldo

2009–2018 · Forward

Four Champions Leagues in five years, including three in a row, and more goals for the club than anyone else has scored. The team was rebuilt around him, and the rebuilding worked.

Luka Modrić

2012–2025 · Midfield

Six Champions Leagues in a career that started in Croatian football and ended as the oldest player to win the competition. The 2018 Ballon d'Or is the small part of the story.

Selection and wording are mine; the records are checkable.
